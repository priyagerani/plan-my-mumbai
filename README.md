# Plan My Mumbai — AI-Native Event Architecture Platform

> The serene, luxury event planner for weddings, milestone birthdays, and community celebrations across Mumbai. Powered by grounded local data and Google Gemini.

## 🌐 Live Website
👉 **Experience the Live App:** [https://plan-my-mumbai.vercel.app](https://plan-my-mumbai.vercel.app)

---

## ✨ Four Core Capabilities

1. **💰 AI Budget Planner with 3-Tier Priorities**
   - Tell the AI your event type, Mumbai locality, guest count, and total budget, and it builds a complete budget breakdown.
   - Segmented priority control (🔒 Non-Negotiable, ⭐ Important, 💸 Flexible) so your must-haves are protected first.
   - Grounded in current Mumbai market rates, with a built-in Budget Reality Check showing transparent trade-offs and savings reasoning.

2. **📅 Dual Actionable Timelines**
   - Multi-week countdown that tells you what to book and finalize, and when.
   - Day-of hour-by-hour run of show for weddings, birthdays, and community events.

3. **✅ Vendor & Venue Checklists**
   - Curated Mumbai venue matching by guest capacity, budget fit, and must-ask questions.
   - Pan-Mumbai catering options with per-plate math and dietary purity badges (Pure Veg & Jain).
   - Shortlist Concierge for assisted inquiry capture, so venue and caterer introductions are one step away.

4. **🎨 Personalized Ideas**
   - Themes, décor, entertainment, games, and cake ideas tailored to the event type, guest profile, and your own notes.
   - Turns "I'm planning X" into a plan that feels like yours, not a generic template.
